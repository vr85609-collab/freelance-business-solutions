# VR Forge Digital · Brand guide

## Identity

- **Name:** VR Forge Digital
- **Short name:** VR Forge
- **Founder:** Victor Rodriguez
- **Core line:** Websites, apps, and systems built around your business.
- **Promise:** Practical digital tools that make it easier to serve customers, track work, and grow.
- **Logo:** Keep the existing blue and violet VR monogram. Pair it with “VR Forge Digital” in text; do not redraw its letters or replace it with a virtual-reality icon.

## Voice

Clear, capable, approachable. Lead with the business problem and a useful outcome. Explain technical work in ordinary language. Avoid unsupported results, invented client testimonials, and claims that a sample dashboard is a live client system.

## Color and typography

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep navy background | Midnight | `#07111F` |
| Primary text | Ice | `#EDF4FA` |
| Supporting text | Slate blue | `#A8BACB` |
| Logo blue | Electric blue | `#27C8FF` |
| Logo violet | Bright violet | `#A349FF` |

Use the current system sans-serif stack. Headlines are bold and compact; body copy stays readable. Blue and violet can appear together in small accents and calls to action, with sufficient contrast for text.

Use “VR Forge Digital” in the website title, description, navigation, footer, demo, project form, campaign captions, social profiles, and future domain/email display.
