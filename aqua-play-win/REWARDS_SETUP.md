# Rewards setup

The previous in-memory demo is superseded by the verified web rewards implementation.
See [REWARDS_AND_PLAY_HANDOFF.md](docs/REWARDS_AND_PLAY_HANDOFF.md) for the complete
configuration, migration, thresholds, customer identity, gift approval, idempotent
Shopify issuance, validation and activation procedure. Redemption remains disabled
by default. Local stars and historical device progress are not redeemable.

The Google Play build is a separately disclosed offline practice edition and does
not include real-value reward redemption. Google Play policy and final business
terms require review before adding such rewards to a Play-distributed game.
