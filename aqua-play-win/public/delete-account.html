Delete your Aqua Arcade rewards account

  1. Open Aqua Arcade and expand Store rewards.
  2. Sign in with your Shopify customer account.
  3. Choose Delete rewards account and confirm.

This deletes arcade points, progress, claims, and sessions. A keyed fraud-prevention hash remains for 365 days, as explained in our privacy notice. Shopify orders and your store account are separate.

If sign-in is unavailable, email aquaemoticonsllc@gmail.com with the subject “Delete Aqua Arcade rewards account.” We will verify the account before processing the request. Do not send your password or payment details.

For the offline Android edition, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove progress. It has no arcade server account.
