Prepared October 30, 2026. Operator: AquaemoticonsLLC. Contact: aquaemoticonsllc@gmail.com.

Games on your device

Practice stars, days played, theme, language, favorites and recent games are saved on your device. Clear browser storage or Android app storage to remove them. We do not sell this information or use advertising trackers in this app.

Optional verified web rewards

Only if you sign in to web rewards, we store your verified Shopify customer ID, login session, game challenge, verified wins, points, days, streaks, gift requests and coupon claims on our server. We do not import browser-local scores as reward credit. Game action sequences are sent for verification and processed in memory; challenge records are kept for up to two days after expiry when maintenance runs. Login sessions expire after eight hours. Your active rewards account and claim records remain until you delete it.

We share the customer ID and discount restrictions with Shopify to issue and redeem coupons. Render hosts the backend and PostgreSQL stores rewards records. Shipping addresses and orders are handled through Shopify checkout; the arcade does not collect addresses, payment cards, microphone, camera or location information.

Deletion

Use Delete rewards account in the web app to delete rewards progress, points, game sessions and claims from the arcade. A one-way keyed hash of the customer ID is retained for 365 days solely to prevent closing and reopening an account to bypass reward limits. The hash is deleted after expiry by maintenance. Previously issued Shopify codes expire as stated. Deleting arcade rewards does not delete Shopify purchase records or your Shopify customer account; contact us for those requests.

Android edition

The prepared Google Play edition bundles games offline. It has no rewards sign-in, coupon redemption, server progress upload, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. Links opened in an external browser are subject to that website's privacy policy. The Play edition's progress is virtual and has no store value.

Private multiplayer rooms / Salas privadas multijugador

Online friend matches process a room code, random player access keys, moves and connection times. Your access key is kept in session storage in your current browser tab to reconnect. No name, email, chat, payments or prize credits are required for multiplayer. Rooms are kept in server memory only, expire after 30 idle minutes or two hours, and disappear on restart. Hosting infrastructure also receives standard network request information. Clear session storage or close the tab to remove your local room key.

Las partidas con amigos procesan códigos de sala, claves aleatorias, jugadas y tiempos de conexión. La clave se guarda en el almacenamiento de sesión de la pestaña para reconectar. No se requieren nombres, correo, chat, pagos ni puntos para premios. Las salas se guardan en memoria temporal, vencen tras 30 minutos inactivas o dos horas y se borran al reiniciar el servidor. El alojamiento también recibe información de red estándar. Cierra la pestaña o borra el almacenamiento de sesión para eliminar la clave local.
