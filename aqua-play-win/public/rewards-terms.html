Aqua Arcade web rewards rules

Draft program configuration, October 30, 2026. Redemption remains unavailable until the operator activates the tested program. These rules apply only to the web rewards program. Android practice stars have no monetary or store value.

No purchase, paid entry, stake, or purchased points are required. Rewards are promotional discounts or product credits; points cannot be transferred, sold, wagered, or exchanged for cash. The operator is AquaemoticonsLLC, aquaemoticonsllc@gmail.com.

Earning verified points

Sign in with a verified Shopify customer account and play online. A server-replayed win earns 20 points, for up to three different games per day. A game must last at least 20 seconds. Bubble Guess and Higher or Lower award practice stars only. Partial 2048 results do not count as wins.

The first verified win on a calendar day adds 5 points and records a day played. Every seventh consecutive day with a verified win adds 25 points. Days and weeks use America/Chicago time. Missing a day resets the current streak; it does not remove previously earned points. Offline, imported, altered and device-local scores are never redeemable. Automation, account sharing and fabricated submissions are not permitted. Replay validation is not a guarantee of bot detection.

Coupon thresholds

  • 1,000 available points, 10 verified wins and 3 days: 5% coupon.
  • 2,500 points, 25 wins and 7 days: 10% coupon.
  • 5,000 points, 50 wins and 14 days: 15% coupon.

Points are spent when a claim is reserved. One coupon or gift code per customer per Monday–Sunday week. Coupons require a $25 qualifying merchandise subtotal, expire 72 hours after issuance is initiated, are restricted to that Shopify customer, have one use in total and cannot combine with other discounts. Shipping and taxes are excluded. Expired issued coupons do not restore spent points. A failed network request may leave points reserved; retry the same claim or contact support with its claim ID. Do not create another account or claim.

Private-label bath-bomb gift requests

At 10,000 points, 100 verified wins and 30 days, you may request a gift review. Points are reserved while the operator reviews your request. Approval is discretionary, is not random and is not a promise of a gift. If rejected, reserved points are returned. Approved customers receive a restricted product credit covering one unit's merchandise price at issuance, capped at $10. Adding more units does not increase the credit. Add the approved variant to your cart and use your code at Shopify checkout. Shipping and taxes are not included; if the price changes after issuance, a difference can remain payable. Codes do not create or ship an order automatically. Gifts depend on approved products and available inventory, with one accepted gift request per active customer account. Deleted accounts cannot reopen rewards for 365 days without operator assistance.

Initial program capacity is 20 issued or in-progress codes per week and 5 gift codes per rolling 30 days. Capacity can pause claims. Eligibility and available points are checked before reservation; checkout remains subject to Shopify's validation. The operator will publish rule changes before activating different thresholds.
