Redemption / Canjes

Coupons are fixed dollar amounts, not percentages, and apply only to the three eligible bath-bomb variants. Only those eligible products count toward the qualifying subtotal. Codes belong to one verified customer, have one total use, expire seven days after issuance begins, and cannot combine with other product, order or shipping discounts. One coupon or gift code per account per Monday–Sunday week. Points are reserved when claimed; expired issued codes do not refund points. Do not start a new claim after a timeout: retry the existing claim or contact support with its ID.

Gift with purchase / Regalo con compra

Reaching 4,000 points, 150 verified wins and 60 active days lets you REQUEST a gift review. Victor chooses whether to approve the player and which of the three bath bombs to gift. Approval is discretionary and is not a random draw or guarantee. Rejected requests return the reserved points. If approved, buy any five eligible bath bombs, then add ONE additional unit of the specific approved gift variant (six total). Checkout discounts one approved unit by 100%, once per order and once per code; buying more does not multiply gifts. The gift is not automatically added to your cart or shipped. Shipping and taxes are not covered. One accepted gift request per rewards account.

Initial capacity: 10 issued or reserved codes per week across the program, including gifts, and 3 gift codes per rolling 30 days. Stock, recorded costs and the reward budget are checked before issuance. Stock is not reserved by a code. Capacity or cost changes may pause new claims without spending new points. If a claim was already reserved, it remains reserved for retry or operator review; it is not automatically refunded while a code may exist. The operator must review active codes before price/cost changes, and will announce future rule changes before activation.
